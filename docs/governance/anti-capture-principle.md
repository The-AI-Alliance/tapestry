# The Anti-Capture Principle

Tapestry's purpose is sovereign AI for its participants. Its governance must honor that purpose: no participant's sovereignty should be compromised by another's power within the platform, and Tapestry itself must never become the dependency it was built to replace.

This principle operates in two directions ([TAP-010](../architecture/decisions/adr-010-open-commons-sovereign-assets.md)):

1. **Capture of the commons** — shared infrastructure, models, standards, governance, or exit must not depend on proprietary permission or unilateral control by any participant, vendor, or operator.
2. **Capture of participants** — participation or access to the Shared Base must not be used to compel disclosure, free licensing, or contribution of sovereign assets beyond what the participant has voluntarily contributed.

A policy satisfies this principle only if it preserves an independently usable open Shared Commons *and* allows participants to withhold, exit with, license, and independently use assets they have not voluntarily contributed to that commons.
